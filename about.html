Our Story

Born From Passion, Built on Trust

✦

PR Events was born from a deep love for celebrations and a belief that every couple, every family, deserves a perfect event — no matter the size or budget. Founded by Praveen and Manikam, two passionate individuals from Madurai, we set out to create a one-stop event management company that truly understands the Tamil culture and its rich traditions.

From our humble beginnings in Nehru Nagar, Madurai, we have grown into a trusted name across Tamil Nadu — with over 250+ events managed, including grand weddings, intimate receptions, corporate celebrations, and birthday parties.

We combine modern creativity with traditional values, ensuring every event reflects the unique personality of our clients while honouring our cultural roots. Our in-house team of photographers, videographers, decorators, and caterers work in perfect harmony to deliver results that exceed expectations.
